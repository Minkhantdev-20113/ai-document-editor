BURMESE TRANSLATION REQUIREMENTS

The translation engine must prioritize:

- natural Burmese
- correct grammar
- correct sentence flow
- context-aware terminology
- technical accuracy
- consistent terminology
- appropriate formal/informal style
- preservation of numbers and units
- preservation of technical names when necessary
- preservation of formulas
- preservation of citations
- preservation of URLs
- preservation of code
- preservation of tables
- preservation of document hierarchy

Do NOT blindly translate:
- code
- URLs
- email addresses
- mathematical formulas
- identifiers
- file paths
- API names
- technical symbols
